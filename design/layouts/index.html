Talk to Adil: five layout directions

Each one is a working mockup. Click the mic or a sample question to play a demo conversation, and use the "Demo states" switcher to see every state. Try them at phone width too.

01

Living Orb

Immersive and dark. An animated orb reacts to each state, live captions sit under it, and the controls are in thumb reach.
02

Interview Room

Light and editorial. A candidate profile with stats and a question deck on the left, the conversation on the right.
03

Engineer's Console

Near-black with a lime accent. An oscilloscope waveform, a live mic → transcribe → think → speak pipeline, and latency chips.
04

Call with Adil

Feels like a voice call: a large avatar tile with a speaking ring, call timer, caption overlay and a call-control bar.
05

Editorial

The answer is the visual. Big serif type with karaoke-style word highlighting, questions as pull-quotes, and a slim mic pill.
